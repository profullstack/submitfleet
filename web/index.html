From one URL to a tracked launch

The slow part of a launch is not deciding to do it. It is filling in the same form a hundred times and losing track of where you already are.

Add your product

Paste your URL. SubmitFleet reads your page and drafts the name, tagline, description and logo. You fix anything that is off and approve it once.

Pick where to launch

Filter the catalog by type, price and audience. Free listings only by default. Anywhere you already submitted is skipped automatically, across every campaign.

Work the queue

Each destination comes with its submit link and every field ready to copy, cut to the length forms expect. Mark it submitted, then live, and keep the listing URL as proof.

A launch queue, not a spreadsheet

See what is waiting for you, what is under review and what is live, for every product you launch. Reports export as Markdown, JSON or CSV.

Where a destination offers an API that allows it, SubmitFleet submits for you, with an approval step first and a receipt after. Everywhere else it does all the preparation and you press the button: usually a minute or two per site.

No double submissions

Proof of what went live
